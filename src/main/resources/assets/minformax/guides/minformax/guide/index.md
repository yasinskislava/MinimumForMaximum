# MinForMax Master Manual

Welcome to the automated frontiers of **MinForMax**. This guidebook is systematically split into dedicated modules to help you design, scale, and optimize your production factory from early-game mob farming to end-game resource production and energy generation.

---

## 📖 Manual Directory

Select a module below to begin your adventure into the world of automation:

### 1. [Mob Farming & Entity Scanning](1_mob_farming.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Entity scanning, shard inscription, and drop production.
* **Primary tools:** <ItemLink id="minformax:scanner" />, <ItemLink id="minformax:index_inscriber" />, and <ItemLink id="minformax:eternal_generator" />.

### 2. [Automation & Resource Production](2_resource_production.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Automated agriculture crops growth, minimalist block/fluid replication machines, and ultimate ore processing factory.
* **Primary tools:** <ItemLink id="minformax:farmer" />, <ItemLink id="minformax:block_replicator" />, and <ItemLink id="minformax:ore_coalescer" />.

### 3. [Logistics Networks & Machine Upgrades](3_logistics_upgrades.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Cable-free machine relay chaining, side configuration scaling, and efficiency upgrades.
* **Primary tools:** <ItemLink id="minformax:configuration_tool" />, Speed Upgrades, and Processing Upgrades.

### 4. [Endgame Energy & Tick Acceleration](4_endgame_power.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Quantum foam conversion and multi-block acceleration dimension.
* **Primary tools:** <ItemLink id="minformax:pandora_box" /> and <ItemLink id="minformax:babylon_key" />.
