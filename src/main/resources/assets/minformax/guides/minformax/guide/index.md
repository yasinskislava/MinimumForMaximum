# MinForMax Master Manual

Welcome to the automated frontiers of **MinForMax**. This guidebook is systematically split into dedicated modules to help you design, scale, and optimize your production factory from early-game genetic harvesting to end-game subatomic reality distortion.

---

## 📖 System Directory

Select a operational chapter below to begin configuring your automated systems:

### 1. [Mob Farming & Genetic Indexing](1_mob_farming.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Genetic scanning, pattern inscription, and drop simulation infrastructure.
* **Primary hardware:** <ItemLink id="minformax:scanner" />, <ItemLink id="minformax:index_inscriber" />, and <ItemLink id="minformax:eternal_generator" />.

### 2. [Automation & Resource Production](2_resource_production.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Automated agriculture rows, minimalist block/fluid replication matrices, and dense mineral refineries.
* **Primary hardware:** <ItemLink id="minformax:farmer" />, <ItemLink id="minformax:block_replicator" />, and <ItemLink id="minformax:ore_coalescer" />.

### 3. [Logistics Networks & Machine Upgrades](3_logistics_upgrades.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Cable-free machine relay daisy-chaining, configuration copying, and modular efficiency arrays.
* **Primary hardware:** <ItemLink id="minformax:configuration_tool" />, Speed Upgrades, and Processing Matrix modules.

### 4. [Endgame Energy & Reality Distortion](4_endgame_power.md)
* **Core Concepts:** Quantum foam alchemy, stress-siphoning generators, and multi-block dimension speed warping.
* **Primary hardware:** <ItemLink id="minformax:pandora_box" /> and <ItemLink id="minformax:babylon_key" />.
